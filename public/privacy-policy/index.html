Privacy Policy

Effective 31 August 2026

Who we are

Haske Global Travel & Tourism L.L.C S.O.C is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Contact info@haskeglobaltravel.com with any privacy question.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, billing details, travel dates, destinations, passenger details, preferences, messages, quotations, booking records and payment status.

How we use information

We use information to answer enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange and manage bookings, take payment, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal or accounting duties.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayTabs and its banking partners through a secure hosted payment page. We receive payment confirmation and transaction references, but we do not collect or store full card details.

Sharing, storage and choices

We share only what is needed with travel suppliers, payment processors, technology providers and authorities. We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only as long as needed. You may ask to access, correct or delete your information by emailing info@haskeglobaltravel.com.
